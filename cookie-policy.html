Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, remember your preferences, and provide insight into how the site is used. This Cookie Policy explains how The Cask Insurance Agency, Inc. ("Cask Agency," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on www.caskagency.com (the "Website").

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the Website to function, such as remembering your light/dark mode preference and enabling secure page navigation.
  • Functional cookies — used by embedded third-party tools, such as our contact form provider, to remember form state and display content correctly.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the Website so we can improve content and performance, if and when such tools are enabled.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Website are set by third-party services we rely on, including:

  • Tally — powers our embedded contact form and may set cookies necessary for form submission and file uploads.
  • Google Fonts — used to load the typefaces on this Website; loading fonts may involve a connection to Google's servers.

These third parties may have their own cookie and privacy practices, which we encourage you to review on their respective websites.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Website, including our contact form.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
