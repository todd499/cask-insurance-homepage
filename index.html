Who We Serve

Built for the Spirits Industry

The Cask Insurance Agency specializes in Property & Casualty (P&C) and Surplus Lines insurance solutions tailored specifically for the spirits industry — covering the people who invest in, store, produce, and finance the world's aging barrel inventory.

As barrels increasingly function as tradable financial assets, coverage has to move at the same speed as the market. That's the gap this platform was built to close.

01 Spirits InvestorsPortfolio coverage for individual and institutional holders
02 Spirits Storage FacilitiesBonded warehouses and rickhouses holding third-party inventory
03 Cask Management CompanyProtecting client assets
04 Spirits LendersCollateral protection for barrel-backed lending books
05 Aging Barrel Inventory & Spirits-Backed AssetsValuation-aware coverage that tracks the asset as it matures

Our Partnership

Powered by DBFEX Insurance Services

Through its exclusive partnership with DBFEX Insurance Services, Inc., creator of a next-generation specialty insurance platform focused on the global spirits industry, The Cask Insurance Agency offers a barrel insurance program with unparalleled flexibility, control, and customization.
