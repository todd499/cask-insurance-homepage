Other Insurance Offered

Complete Coverage Beyond the Barrel

Alongside our specialty barrel and spirits programs, we also place the standard commercial lines that protect the rest of your operation. Here are examples of additional coverage you may need:

01 General Liability Insurance for Businesses

General liability insurance is one of the most essential coverages that a business can carry. It protects your company from the financial fallout of common accidents, lawsuits and third-party claims that could otherwise jeopardize your operations.

02 Equipment Breakdown Coverage

Equipment breakdown coverage for business pays to repair or replace vital machinery, computers, and electrical systems damaged by sudden internal failures like power surges or motor burnouts. Standard commercial property insurance excludes these mechanical events, but this coverage adds protection for repair costs, lost income, and spoiled inventory.

03 Commercial Property Coverage

Commercial property insurance protects a business's physical assets—including owned or rented buildings, equipment, inventory, and furniture—from unexpected events like fires, storms, or theft. It often includes business interruption coverage to replace lost income if operations halt due to property damage.

04 Commercial Auto Insurance

Commercial auto insurance protects vehicles owned or used by a business.

05 Workers' Compensation

Workers' compensation insurance is a state-mandated, no-fault system that provides medical benefits and wage replacement to employees who suffer job-related injuries or illnesses.

06 Liquor Liability Insurance

Liquor liability insurance is a specific type of commercial coverage that protects businesses if an intoxicated customer causes bodily injury or property damage after being served alcohol. Standard general liability policies exclude alcohol-related incidents for businesses that profit from drink sales, making standalone policies or specific endorsements vital.

07 Product Recall Insurance

Product recall insurance is a specialized business policy that covers the direct financial costs of removing a defective, contaminated, or dangerous product from the market.

08 Spoilage and Contamination Insurance

Protecting against losses from spoiled or contaminated products is the essential role of spoilage and contamination insurance for distilleries. This type of insurance provides coverage for losses like loss of inventory, loss of income, and additional expenses incurred to replace or repair the affected products.
