Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Our Commitment

The Cask Insurance Agency, Inc. ("Cask Agency," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We are continually working to improve the usability and accessibility of our website at www.caskagency.com (the "Website") for everyone, including individuals who rely on assistive technology.

2. Conformance Standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more usable for everyone.

3. Accessibility Features

We have taken the following measures to support accessibility on the Website:

  • Semantic HTML structure with descriptive headings and landmarks
  • A "Skip to content" link for keyboard and screen reader users
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and icons
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background, including a dark mode option
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, forms, and expandable content
  • Responsive layout that adapts to different screen sizes and zoom levels

4. Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the Website may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively working to identify and remediate any such issues. If you encounter a barrier while using the Website, please let us know using the contact information below so we can address it.

5. Compatibility with Assistive Technology

The Website is designed to be compatible with recent versions of major browsers and commonly used assistive technologies, including screen readers and keyboard-only navigation.

6. Feedback and Assistance

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the Website. If you experience any difficulty accessing content or would like to request information in an alternative format, please contact us at info@caskagency.com or (480) 944-0866. We will do our best to respond promptly and provide the information, item, or service you seek through an alternate communication method.

7. Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We periodically review the Website and make updates as needed to maintain and improve accessibility as standards, technologies, and content evolve.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Accessibility Statement, please contact us at info@caskagency.com or (480) 944-0866, or write to us at 7835 E Evans Road, Suite 300, Scottsdale, AZ 85260.
